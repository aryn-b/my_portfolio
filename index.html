A little about me

My journey into technology started at a young age, huddled over my mother’s old laptop. Long before I knew what a compiler was, I kept messing with the command line, blindly testing different commands I searched up on the internet out of pure curiosity.That childhood tinkering shaped my entire approach to computer science. Today, I bring that same relentless curiosity and self-taught resilience to my program.

My background

I am a first-year Computer Science student at the University of the Cordilleras, currently pursuing a Bachelor of Science in Computer Science. I was born and raised in Kapangan, Benguet, and now live in La Trinidad, Benguet.I am a part of the Kankanaey ethnic group, one of the Indigenous peoples collectively known as Igorot, originating from the Cordillera region of Northern Luzon.

Outside academics, I have a strong interest in digital illustration and visual arts. I enjoy expressing ideas and storytelling through digital artwork, which has also influenced my appreciation for creativity and visual design.

As I continue developing my knowledge in Computer Science, I aspire to explore opportunities where I can combine my technical and creative interests, particularly in areas such as game development, UI/UX design, and cloud computing.
